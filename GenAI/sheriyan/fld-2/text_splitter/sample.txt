**Company HR Policy Manual**
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026

---

### 1. Introduction

This Human Resources (HR) Policy Manual outlines the guidelines, rules, and expectations for all employees of the organization. It is designed to ensure a professional, respectful, and productive work environment.

---

### 2. Employment Policies

#### 2.1 Equal Opportunity Employment

The company provides equal employment opportunities to all employees and applicants regardless of race, gender, religion, age, disability, or any other protected status.

#### 2.2 Employment Types

* Full-time Employees
* Part-time Employees
* Contract Employees
* Interns

---

### 3. Working Hours

* Standard working hours: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM (Monday to Friday)
* Employees are expected to be punctual and maintain attendance.
* Flexible work arrangements may be granted based on role and manager approval.

---

### 4. Leave Policy

#### 4.1 Paid Leave

* 18 days of annual paid leave
* Leave must be approved in advance

#### 4.2 Sick Leave

* 10 days per year
* Medical certificate required for more than 3 consecutive days

#### 4.3 Casual Leave

* 7 days per year

---

### 5. Code of Conduct

Employees are expected to:

* Maintain professionalism at all times
* Respect colleagues and workplace diversity
* Avoid harassment or discrimination
* Follow company policies and procedures

---

### 6. Compensation and Benefits

* Salaries are paid monthly
* Performance-based bonuses may be provided
* Health insurance coverage is included
* Retirement benefits as per company policy

---

### 7. Performance Management

* Employees will undergo performance reviews twice a year
* Feedback will be provided by managers
* Promotions and increments are based on performance

---

### 8. Disciplinary Actions

Violation of company policies may result in:

* Verbal warning
* Written warning
* Suspension
* Termination

---

### 9. Confidentiality Policy

Employees must not disclose confidential company information, including:

* Business strategies
* Client data
* Financial information

---

### 10. Termination Policy

* Employees must provide a 30-day notice period
* The company reserves the right to terminate employment for misconduct or policy violations

---

### 11. Remote Work Policy

* Employees may work remotely with approval
* Must maintain productivity and availability during work hours

---

### 12. IT and Security Policy

* Use company systems responsibly
* Do not install unauthorized software
* Protect login credentials

---

### 13. Grievance Handling

Employees can report concerns to HR without fear of retaliation. All complaints will be handled confidentially.

---

### 14. Policy Updates

The company reserves the right to update policies at any time. Employees will be notified of any changes.

---

**Acknowledgment**
By continuing employment, employees agree to abide by all company policies outlined in this document.
